# TCS — Account & Data Deletion

TCS (Taylors College Social) lets verified Taylors College Sydney students
and staff request deletion of their account and associated personal data.

## How to request deletion

Email **yigamatthewdale285@gmail.com** with the subject
**"TCS account deletion request"**, and include:

- your full name as shown in the app, and
- your Student or Staff ID.

We verify the request against the account and then delete it.

## What is deleted

On a verified request we delete the personal data associated with your
account, including:

- your profile (name, ID, date of birth, avatar, bio, interests, links)
- your posts, comments, photos, videos and voice notes
- your chat messages and direct messages
- your game stats, XP and tokens
- your device push-notification token

## What may be retained

We may retain limited information where required for security, fraud
prevention, or to meet legal obligations, for no longer than necessary,
after which it is deleted.

## Timeline

Verified deletion requests are completed within 30 days.
